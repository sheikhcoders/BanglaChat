## 2026-04-10 - Establishing Bilingual Foundation in Uninitialized Repos
**Learning:** In uninitialized repositories, the `README.md` is the primary interface. Using standard Markdown headers with bilingual content (English/Bengali) and wrapping them in `<div lang="...">` with `role="region"` and `aria-label` ensures accessibility for screen readers while maintaining visual consistency.
**Action:** Always establish a bilingual, accessible `README.md` as the first UX improvement in projects targeting specific language communities.
