## 2026-07-20 - Establishing Inclusive Foundations in Uninitialized Repositories
**Learning:** In uninitialized repositories lacking functional application code, documentation and contribution templates serve as the primary micro-UX surfaces; prioritizing accessibility in these early artifacts establishes an inclusive tone for the project's lifecycle.
**Action:** Always start with bilingual accessibility (English/Bengali) and include accessibility validation prompts in developer contribution templates from day one.
