## 2026-02-27 - Foundational Bilingual UX

**Learning:** For a localized project like BanglaChat, bilingual documentation (README) and a contribution process (PR Template) that enforces accessibility are not just "nice to have"—they are foundational UX requirements. They set the tone for an inclusive development culture from day one.

**Action:** Always initialize localized repositories with bilingual support and automated/manual accessibility checks in the PR workflow to ensure the mission of inclusivity is maintained.
