## 2025-05-22 - Bilingual Accessible Documentation

**Learning:** For localized projects like BanglaChat, providing equivalent content in both English and Bengali from the start sets a standard for inclusivity. Wrapping expressive emojis in `<span role="img" aria-label="...">` ensures that the "delight" aspect of UX is also accessible to screen reader users.
**Action:** Apply the 'Bilingual Accessible Documentation' pattern to all core repository documents (README, PR templates, etc.) using a consistent side-by-side or stacked layout.
