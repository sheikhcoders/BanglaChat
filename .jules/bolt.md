## 2026-05-09 - CI Compute Optimization for Uninitialized Repositories
**Learning:** In uninitialized repositories (lacking `package.json`), standard CI workflows for Node.js and Next.js can consume unnecessary compute minutes or fail due to missing dependencies. Implementing job-level guards (`if: hashFiles('package.json') != ''`), path filtering (`paths-ignore`), and concurrency controls (`cancel-in-progress`) can save significant CI resources (estimated ~7 minutes per redundant run). Additionally, `actions/setup-node@v4` caching must be conditional on lockfile existence to prevent fatal errors.
**Action:** Always implement job-level guards and path filtering for non-code files (README, journals) in early-stage or uninitialized repositories to preserve CI quota and avoid noisy failure alerts.
