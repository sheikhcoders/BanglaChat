## 2026-04-09 - CI Optimization in Uninitialized Repositories
**Learning:** In repositories that are still in their bootstrap phase (missing `package.json` or lockfiles), CI workflows require specific hardening to remain performant without failing. Using `paths-ignore` for documentation and `concurrency: cancel-in-progress: true` provides immediate resource savings. However, when hardening for missing lockfiles, it's critical to prioritize `npm ci` for speed while falling back to `npm install` only when necessary, rather than defaulting to `npm install` for everyone.
**Action:** Always prefer `npm ci` in CI environments for performance. Use `hashFiles('package-lock.json')` to conditionally choose between `ci` and `install` to maintain both speed and compatibility with early-stage projects.
