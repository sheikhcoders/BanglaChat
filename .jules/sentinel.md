## 2025-03-04 - Established Security Baseline
**Vulnerability:** Initial repository state lacked explicit security policy and hardened CI workflows, increasing the risk of uncoordinated disclosure and potential CI-based attacks.
**Learning:** For a new or growing project, establishing a security baseline (SECURITY.md and CI hardening) is crucial for defense-in-depth and building trust with contributors.
**Prevention:** Always initialize repositories with a SECURITY.md and enforce least-privilege permissions in all CI/CD workflows from the start.
